## Запрос

Use case: precise-object-edit.
Asset type: photographic reflection texture for a real interactive whiteboard app.
Input image 1 is the exact lighting reference. Create ONLY the empty inner enamel surface of this whiteboard with EXACTLY the same photographic window reflection. Remove ALL writing, all sticky notes, icons, dividing lines, metal frame, tray, pens and all UI. Fill their former areas naturally with the same empty glossy enamel and continuous reflection. Crop flush to the enamel surface, landscape 16:10.
Crucial: match the reference's actual reflection: a large white window reflected at the LEFT edge, near-vertical slightly perspective slanted white mullions, diagonal horizontal crossbars, sunny softly defocused pale yellow-green living tree foliage seen THROUGH its glass especially at far left and lower left. Farther toward center the reflection dissolves naturally into light gray-white enamel. Bottom right has the very soft diagonal sunlight/mullion glints seen in reference, but no foliage there. Broad diffuse daylight, subtle surface sheen, calm natural photographic realism. Keep visible softly focused green leaf shapes at left (not gray leaf silhouettes/shadows). Window reflection is luminous, white frame with soft gray edges, never dark black grid lines. Keep central and right 65% quiet neutral pearl-gray-white enamel. No curtains. No typography. No cards. No drawn illustration, no artificial repeating gradient stripes, no hard geometry grid. This is a real reflected room window and outdoor foliage, not a view directly through a window and not a wall with projected leaf shadows. All movement will be added by code later.
